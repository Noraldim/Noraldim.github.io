2025 – 2026 · Senior capstone · Computer vision

AI-Driven Urban Planner

From satellite pixels to a city plan. Deep segmentation finds land that is free to build on, then an automated planner lays out a whole district on top of it.

  • Segments 10 m Sentinel-2 imagery. Seven U-Net and DeepLabV3+ models are compared on a binary open-land dataset derived from PASTIS.
  • The best model (DeepLabV3+ with ResNet-101) reaches a 0.942 overall score and 0.937 Dice, well above the 0.725 of a trivial baseline.
  • A rule-based, conflict-free planner places hospitals, stadiums, malls, apartments and houses. A 236-building plan fits completely on 84% of tiles.
  • Results are rendered as an interactive 3D web visualization.
