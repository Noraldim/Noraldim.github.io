Automated Diagnosis of Plant Leaf Diseases Using Machine Learning

Read the paperDownload PDFSource code

What it is about

Can a neural network tell a healthy leaf from a sick one? This project builds the whole pipeline from scratch on two very different sources. One is photos taken with a phone in the author’s own garden, under changing light and on natural backgrounds. The other is the clean, labelled PlantVillage dataset from Kaggle. The report walks through every step, from the raw photo to the predicted class.

2datasets: home garden and PlantVillage
128×128normalised grayscale input
4hand-crafted features per region of interest
≈60%test accuracy

What the paper covers

  • Image collection and preprocessing: resizing to a fixed 128×128, grayscale conversion and normalisation to [0, 1].
  • Labelling and one-hot encoding of disease classes, and how an image becomes a NumPy matrix the model can read.
  • Region-of-interest selection and feature extraction: pixel mean and variance, Canny edge density, colour histogram and contour count.
  • Training a multi-layer neural network and testing it on images it has never seen.
  • An honest observation: the model does better on the standardised PlantVillage images than on the garden photos, because real-world lighting and image quality vary much more.
